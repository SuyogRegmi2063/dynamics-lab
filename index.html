A suggested path

  1. Read lessons 1–3, then play with Simulation 1 in free-vibration mode. Change the damping and watch the decay.
  2. Read lesson 4, then find resonance in Simulation 2 and compare with the Harmonic response calculator.
  3. Work through lessons 5–6 and reproduce a pulse response with the Newmark solver in the Toolbox.
  4. Study lessons 7–8, then check your hand calculation of a 2-storey frame with the modal analysis tool.
  5. Revise from your notes and past year questions.
  6. Finish with the quiz.
