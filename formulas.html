SDOF basics lessons 1–3

QuantityFormula
Equation of motion\(m\ddot u+c\dot u+ku=p(t)\)
Natural frequency\(\omega_n=\sqrt{k/m}\)
Frequency / period\(f_n=\omega_n/2\pi,\ T_n=1/f_n\)
Critical damping\(c_{cr}=2m\omega_n,\ \xi=c/c_{cr}\)
Damped frequency\(\omega_D=\omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2}\)
Log decrement\(\delta=\dfrac{2\pi\xi}{\sqrt{1-\xi^2}}\approx2\pi\xi\)
Column stiffness\(k=12EI/h^3\) (fixed–fixed), \(3EI/h^3\) (cantilever)

Harmonic loading lesson 4

QuantityFormula
Frequency ratio\(r=\bar\omega/\omega_n\)
Amplification factor\(D=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{(1-r^2)^2+(2\xi r)^2}}\)
Phase lag\(\theta=\tan^{-1}\dfrac{2\xi r}{1-r^2}\)
Resonance\(D_{r=1}=1/(2\xi)\)
Peak of Dat \(r=\sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\), \(D_{max}=\dfrac{1}{2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi^2}}\)
Transmissibility\(TR=D\sqrt{1+(2\xi r)^2}\), isolation if \(r\gt\sqrt2\)

Arbitrary loads lesson 5

QuantityFormula
Duhamel integral\(u=\dfrac{1}{m\omega_D}\displaystyle\int_0^t p(\tau)e^{-\xi\omega_n(t-\tau)}\sin\omega_D(t-\tau)d\tau\)
Step load (undamped)\(u=\dfrac{p_0}{k}(1-\cos\omega_nt)\), peak \(=2p_0/k\)
Newmark, average accel.\(\beta=\tfrac14,\ \gamma=\tfrac12\), unconditionally stable
Newmark, linear accel.\(\beta=\tfrac16,\ \gamma=\tfrac12\), stable if \(\Delta t/T_n\le0.551\)
Accuracy guide\(\Delta t\le T_n/10\)

Earthquake loading lesson 6

QuantityFormula
Base excitation\(m\ddot u+c\dot u+ku=-m\ddot u_g\)
Pseudo-acceleration\(S_a=\omega_n^2S_d\)
Base shear\(V_b=mS_a=kS_d\)
Combine modesSRSS: \(r=\sqrt{\sum r_n^2}\), or CQC for close modes

MDOF systems lessons 7–8

QuantityFormula
Equation of motion\(\mathbf M\ddot{\mathbf u}+\mathbf C\dot{\mathbf u}+\mathbf K\mathbf u=\mathbf p(t)\)
Eigenproblem\((\mathbf K-\omega^2\mathbf M)\boldsymbol\phi=0\)
Orthogonality\(\boldsymbol\phi_i^T\mathbf M\boldsymbol\phi_j=0,\ i\ne j\)
Modal mass\(M_n=\boldsymbol\phi_n^T\mathbf M\boldsymbol\phi_n\)
Participation factor\(\Gamma_n=\boldsymbol\phi_n^T\mathbf M\boldsymbol\iota/M_n\)
Effective modal mass\(M_n^*=(\boldsymbol\phi_n^T\mathbf M\boldsymbol\iota)^2/M_n\), \(\sum M_n^*=\sum m\)
Rayleigh damping\(\mathbf C=a_0\mathbf M+a_1\mathbf K,\ \xi_n=\dfrac{a_0}{2\omega_n}+\dfrac{a_1\omega_n}{2}\)
Uniform shear building\(\omega_n=2\sqrt{k/m}\,\sin\dfrac{(2n-1)\pi}{2(2N+1)}\)

Beams lesson 9

QuantityFormula
Governing equation\(EI\,y''''+\bar m\,\ddot y=0\)
Simply supported\(\omega_n=(n\pi/L)^2\sqrt{EI/\bar m}\)
Cantilever\(\omega_n=\lambda_n^2\sqrt{EI/(\bar mL^4)}\), \(\lambda_n=1.875,\ 4.694,\ 7.855\)
Rayleigh quotient\(\omega^2\approx\dfrac{\int EI(\psi'')^2dx}{\int\bar m\psi^2dx}\)
